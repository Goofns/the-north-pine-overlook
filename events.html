Events Near Bailey, Colorado

Festivals, live music, fairs, and state park programs around Bailey and the 285 corridor, kept current so you can plan around them.

The North Pine Overlook  /  Events Near Bailey

One of the good things about a small mountain town is that when something is happening, the whole canyon shows up. Below is what is dated and coming up around Bailey and along the Highway 285 corridor, plus the things that happen on a regular rhythm all season. We keep it current, and we take things off once they have passed.

Upcoming events

Dated, one-time happenings worth building a day around.

August 4

National Night Out in Fairplay. The county seat's annual community night brings out free food and family activities in the town center as a way for neighbors to meet up and get to know local first responders. It is a relaxed, low-key stop if you are passing through Fairplay on a summer evening, about thirty minutes past Bailey on 285.

Source: Explore Park County
August 8

Adaptive recreation day at Staunton State Park. Colorado Parks and Wildlife hosts a midday event where visitors can learn about adaptive recreation programs and try adaptive equipment firsthand. It runs late morning into early afternoon and is open to anyone curious, not only registered participants.

Source: Colorado Parks and Wildlife
August 8 and 9

Living History Days at South Park City Museum. The open air museum in Fairplay, built from more than forty original 1880s buildings, brings its frontier trades to life for a weekend with costumed demonstrations of cooking, blacksmithing, and daily mining town life. It is about thirty minutes past Bailey on 285, an easy add on to a history minded day.

Source: Explore Park County
August 11 to 13

Junior Rangers at Staunton State Park. Colorado Parks and Wildlife runs a hands-on outdoor program for kids at Staunton across three mornings, with climbing, fishing, archery, and hiking. It is about fifteen minutes from the cabin and registration is required, so sign up ahead if you are traveling with young ones.

Source: Colorado Parks and Wildlife
August 22

Staunton Rocks Marathon and Half. Trail runners take on the park's singletrack for a full marathon or a half marathon, starting and finishing near the Ranch Hand trailhead. The trails stay open to regular hikers and bikers too, but expect a fuller parking lot and more foot traffic that morning, so plan around it if you are headed in for a quiet walk.

Source: Gnar Runners
August 22 and 23

Evergreen Fine Arts Festival. This is the 60th year for this juried art show at Buchanan Fields in Evergreen, with painting, jewelry, and sculpture alongside local music and food. It runs about forty five minutes from the cabin, and a free shuttle helps with parking, which fills up fast on a nice August weekend.

Source: Evergreen Fine Arts Festival
August 28

TGIFairplay free concert series. Fairplay closes off part of downtown for a free evening concert, this round pairing a live band with a peach and ice cream social. It runs early evening and draws a good local crowd, so bring a chair if you want to sit close, and expect a lively Main Street rather than a quick drive through.

Source: Explore Park County
Labor Day weekend, early September

Bailey Fest. The town's big one. Live music across several days in Bailey, with camping on site and a crowd that comes in from all over the Front Range. If you are staying that weekend, plan around it rather than through it, since lodging in the canyon fills up.

Source: Explore Park County
September 12

Big Chili Cook-Off. Evergreen's community chili competition and music festival at Buchanan Park raises money for the local mountain fire departments, with tasting, live entertainment, and a firefighter challenge for kids. It draws a big crowd, so plan to arrive early since chili tasting runs out by midafternoon.

Source: Big Chili Cook-Off

Repeating events

Things that happen on a steady rhythm all season, not tied to one date.

Most Saturdays and Sundays

Live music at Aspen Peak Cellars. The winery and bistro right on US 285 in Bailey puts live music on its riverside patio most weekend afternoons, roughly two to five, alongside the North Fork of the South Platte. It is a short drive from the cabin and an easy way to spend a Saturday, though the patio fills up when the weather is nice, so a table reservation is worth doing ahead.

Source: Aspen Peak Cellars
Mid to late September

Aspen season on Kenosha Pass. Not an event exactly, but the biggest thing on the local calendar. The aspen stands along US 285 at Kenosha Pass turn gold, usually peaking mid to late September, and weekend traffic on the pass gets heavy. Weekday mornings are the quiet way to see it.

Source: US Forest Service, Pike San Isabel

Where these come from

We pull from Explore Park County, the Platte Canyon area chamber, Colorado Parks and Wildlife program listings for Staunton State Park, and the local places that host things: Aspen Peak Cellars, Mad Jack's Mountain Brewery, the Sasquatch Outpost, and the Park County Fair. Dates and details change, especially for small-town events, so treat this as a starting point and confirm with the organizer before you build a day around it.

Planning around the calendar

Two patterns worth knowing. Summer weekends bring festivals and live music, and the canyon gets busy. Mid to late September is aspen season, the single busiest stretch of the year on the 285 corridor, so book early and drive the passes on weekday mornings if you can. Winter is quiet by comparison, which for a lot of people is the point.

Skiing Near Bailey, Colorado

Eight ski areas within reach of the cabin, from a quick evening run to a full resort day, with addresses, drive times, and hours.

Mountain hours, terrain, and schedules change through the season — check each resort's current conditions and hours before you drive up.

1. Echo Mountain

Address19285 CO-103, Idaho Springs, CO 80452
Drive time~50 min–1 hour (via US-285 N to Evergreen, then Squaw Pass Rd/CO-103)
HoursMon–Tue 10am–5pm, Wed–Fri 10am–7pm, Sat 9am–7pm, Sun 9am–5pm

The closest ski area to Denver and Bailey. Small and beginner-friendly with night skiing, tubing lanes, and low crowds — great for a quick lesson or evening run rather than a full resort day.

2. Ski Cooper

Address232 Co Rd 29, Leadville, CO 80461
Drive time~1 hr 15–1 hr 30 (via US-285 S through Fairplay, then US-24 to Leadville)
HoursTypically 9am–4pm daily (check seasonal hours)

An affordable, laid-back independent mountain near Leadville with mostly green and blue terrain — a good pick for families and beginners, with far shorter lift lines than the I-70 resorts.

3. Arapahoe Basin

AddressKeystone, CO 80435
Drive time~1 hr 20–1 hr 40 (via Fairplay/Hoosier Pass or via C-470 to I-70 W)
HoursThu–Sun 9am–5pm (closed Mon–Wed in early/late season; check current schedule)

Known for having the longest season in Colorado, "A-Basin" offers steep, advanced terrain and legendary tree runs in Zuma Bowl, with a laid-back, uncrowded vibe.

4. Loveland Ski Area

AddressI-70 Exit 216, Georgetown, CO 80444
Drive time~1 hr 30 (via C-470 to I-70 W)
HoursGenerally 9am–4pm daily in season (hours vary)

A locals' favorite right off I-70, known for reasonable prices, great beginner terrain, and easy access to above-treeline bowls without the crowds of the bigger resorts.

5. Keystone Resort

AddressKeystone, CO 80435
Drive time~1 hr 30–1 hr 45
HoursTypically 8:30am–4pm daily (check seasonal hours)

A large, family-oriented resort with three mountains, extensive night skiing, and some of the best back-bowl terrain in the state when conditions are good.

6. Breckenridge Ski Resort

AddressBreckenridge, CO 80424
Drive time~1 hr 15–1 hr 45 (fastest via Hwy 9 through Fairplay/Alma over Hoosier Pass)
HoursSlopes typically 8:30am–4pm daily

One of Colorado's most popular resorts, with five peaks of varied terrain and a charming, walkable mountain town at the base — expect bigger crowds, especially on weekends.

7. Copper Mountain

AddressFrisco, CO 80443
Drive time~1 hr 30–1 hr 45 (via I-70 W)
HoursMon–Wed 9am–4pm, Thu 9am–5:30pm, Fri–Sun 9am–4pm

Nicknamed "The Athlete's Mountain," Copper's terrain is naturally divided by ability level (beginner, intermediate, expert), making it easy to find the right run without crossing paths with skiers of very different levels.

8. Winter Park Resort

Address85 Parsenn Rd, Winter Park, CO 80482
Drive time~1 hr 45–2 hrs (via C-470, I-70 W, then US-40 over Berthoud Pass)
HoursMon–Fri 9am–4pm, Sat–Sun 8:30am–4pm

A large, varied resort with terrain for every level plus Trestle Bike Park in summer. The Berthoud Pass drive is scenic but can be slow in heavy snow.

Know before you go

Drive times above assume clear roads and normal conditions — winter storms, chain-law closures, and I-70 traffic can add significant time, especially on weekends. Check CDOT's current road conditions and each resort's snow report, lift status, and parking or reservation requirements before heading up.

The North Pine Overlook  /  Rates & Availability

The cabin sleeps 6, with three bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms. It is still being renovated, so we are not publishing rates or promising final amenities yet. We will confirm the complete stay details before accepting reservations.

Opening list

Join the opening list and we'll email you when the finished amenities, policies, rates, and calendar are confirmed.

What's included

The final included-amenities list will be published after installation and inspection. Until then, the home-page preview shows the renovation plan, not a promise for a bookable stay.

Arrival and departure

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is by 10:00 AM.

Accessibility note

Lower-level amenities require stairs. A complete accessibility description is still being prepared; ask about any access need before making a reservation.

Nightly rates

Rates and dates have not been published. When the calendar opens, this page will show the nightly price, available dates, required fees, taxes, and any stay restrictions.

Minimum stay

Minimum-stay rules have not been finalized. The expected setup is a two-night minimum, with possible one-night openings for close-in unbooked dates and possible three-night minimums during select high-demand periods. Final rules will be posted before reservations are accepted.

Fees and taxes

Pets are allowed. A pet fee and cleaning fee will apply, but the final amounts and detailed pet cleanup rules are still being finalized. Airbnb and Vrbo present taxes through their checkout flows after the applicable tax settings are configured. The complete price and policies will be provided in writing before a direct booking is accepted.

Cancellation policy

Cancellation terms are not final and may differ between direct bookings, Airbnb, and Vrbo because each platform limits the available policy options. The expected policy is a full refund outside 30 days before check-in, a 50% refund inside 30 days, and no refund inside two weeks, but the applicable written policy will be confirmed before payment.

How to book

Email honkytonkiesproperties@gmail.com with your proposed dates for a direct-booking inquiry. Availability, price, policies, and payment instructions will be confirmed manually. An inquiry does not reserve dates; a date is reserved only after payment is received and the reservation is confirmed in writing.
